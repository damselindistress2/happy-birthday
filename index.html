baklava recipe.txt
Ingredients:

- 500 g filo pastry
- 250 g walnuts
- 100 g almonds
- 150 g butter
- 150 g sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon

For the syrup:

- 200 g sugar
- 150 ml water
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions:

1. Chop the walnuts and almonds and mix with cinnamon.

2. Melt the butter.

3. Layer the filo pastry in a baking dish, brushing each layer with melted butter.

4. Add the nut mixture between the layers.

5. Finish with several layers of buttered filo.

6. Cut into squares before baking.

7. Bake at 180°C until golden brown.

8. Heat the syrup ingredients together and simmer for a few minutes.

9. Pour the hot syrup over the freshly baked baklava.

10. Let it cool before serving.

Enjoy!
